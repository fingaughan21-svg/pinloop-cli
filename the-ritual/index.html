Prepare the ritual exactly as written. Once he is inside, survive until 3:33 a.m.

The Midnight Game

Copied by hand from the thread. Do not skip a step.

Before midnight

  1. Write your full name (first, middle and last) on a piece of paper.
  2. Put at least one drop of your own blood on it and let it soak in.
  3. Turn off every light in the house.
  4. At your wooden front door, lay the paper down, set a candle on it and light it.
  5. Knock 22 times. The last knock must land at exactly 12:00 a.m.
  6. Open the door, blow out the candle, close the door. You have let the Midnight Man in.
  7. Immediately relight your candle. The game has begun.

Until 3:33 a.m.

  • Wander the dark house with your candle. Avoid the Midnight Man at all costs.
  • If the candle goes out, he is near. Relight it within ten seconds.
  • If you cannot, immediately surround yourself with a circle of salt and stay inside it until 3:33.
  • Fail both, and he will show you your greatest fear until 3:33.
  • Signs he is close: a sudden cold, a pure black figure in the dark, soft whispering. Move away.
  • Staying in one spot will only let him find you. Keep moving.

Never

  • Never turn on a light.
  • Never use a flashlight.
  • Never go to sleep.
  • Never use another person's blood on your name.
  • Never use a lighter in place of the candle. It will not work.
  • Never provoke the Midnight Man in any way.

To win, last until 3:33 a.m. without being caught or trapped in the salt.
